## Welcome to the Left Lens Series!

This series is designed for anyone new to left-wing ideas and looking for a clear, accessible starting point. We believe that understanding these concepts can provide a powerful new lens through which to see and understand the world around you, from your daily life to global events.

Our goal is to break down complex topics into understandable parts, avoid jargon where possible, and show how these ideas connect to real-world experiences. Each article builds on the last, so we recommend reading them in order for the best understanding.

We hope this series empowers you with new knowledge and encourages further exploration!

---

### Articles in this Series:

1.  **[Why Do We Talk About Class? Understanding Your Economic Place in the World](/left-lens-1-understanding-class/)**
    *   *A beginner's guide to understanding economic class in the 21st century, exploring the difference between workers and owners, and why it matters for your everyday life.*

2.  **[What is Capitalism? A Simple Guide to the System We Live In](/left-lens-2-what-is-capitalism/)**
    *   *A beginner-friendly explanation of capitalism: what it is, how it works (including where profits come from), and its core principles like competition and the profit motive.*

3.  **[The Left/Right Spectrum: What Does It Actually Mean?](/left-lens-3-left-right-spectrum/)**
    *   *A beginner's explanation of the Left/Right political spectrum, its historical origins, and what it tells us about different views on equality, hierarchy, and social change.*

4.  **[Introducing Socialism: Beyond the Myths and Misconceptions](/left-lens-4-introducing-socialism/)**
    *   *A beginner's guide to understanding socialism, debunking common myths, and explaining its core ideas like democratic control of the economy and prioritizing human needs.*

5.  **[Power and a Paycheck: How Capitalism Shapes Your Daily Work Life](/left-lens-5-capitalism-work-life/)**
    *   *Exploring how capitalism and class dynamics influence your daily job, from wages and workplace power to stress and job security. Connecting big ideas to your everyday life.*
